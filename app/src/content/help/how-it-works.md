# How Private Floor works

These notes describe the product. They are not legal, tax, or investment advice. A Private Floor administrator reviews every membership. Nothing on this page promises an approval.

## Signup

Private Floor is a private network where every member is personally reviewed. Anyone can request to join, and every request is personally reviewed. An applicant can use an invite code, then completes the application at Apply. The form asks for a real name, role, company, and location. A business card image is optional. Private Floor does not ask for a government ID, a Social Security number, or a date of birth. Two consent boxes start unchecked. Both must be checked before the application can be submitted. After submit, the applicant keeps a status link. The status page is where a reply goes if a Private Floor administrator asks for something more.

## Verification

A Private Floor administrator reviews every application. Automation can only recommend. It does not approve anyone. Members and applicants see Verified or Verification in progress. They do not see a score, a source list, or an internal checklist. A Verified badge is a limited review. It is not a guarantee, an endorsement, or due diligence. A declined application does not include the reasons. If you think something is wrong, email connect@privatefloorclub.com with the subject line Verification Review.

## Posts

Approved members can post on the floor, including seeking and raising notes. A seeking or raising post requires the rules acknowledgement. Posts that mention offering terms, a target return, a guarantee, past performance, or a subscription are held for a Private Floor administrator. That hold is not legal advice and is not approval of an offering. Nothing on Private Floor is an offer to sell a security.

## Introductions and messages

Members introduce themselves inside the portal before a thread opens. The person who receives the introduction accepts or declines it. After an acceptance, messages stay on Private Floor. Other members do not see your email, phone, or LinkedIn. You can block someone and you can report a message. A Private Floor administrator reviews reports.

## Deal rooms

The member who created a deal room decides who may enter. Before entering, a member acknowledges the Deal Room NDA. The acknowledgement stores the document version and the time. Documents in a deal room are described by their file name and type. Private Floor does not keep the file bytes. The owner can remove someone. Materials in a deal room are confidential.

## Dues

Developers, investors and LPs, family offices, and lenders pay no monthly dues and become active when a Private Floor administrator approves them. General contractors: $149 per month, first month $74.50. Brokers: $129 per month, first month $64.50. Attorneys and advisors: $129 per month, first month $64.50. Membership is month to month, with no long-term contract, and you can cancel anytime. The first month is 50% off, then the full monthly price. Dues are due only after approval. They are never based on a deal. Checkout is Stripe test mode or a labelled simulated payment. No card number is stored. No live card is charged. An administrator can waive dues for a person. A waiver is not automatic.

## Getting a person

Help answers only from Private Floor's pages. It cannot give legal, tax, or investment advice, and it cannot see an application file. Email connect@privatefloorclub.com.
