# Private Floor Club - Seeking and Raising Post Rules

DRAFT - for attorney review, not legal advice. This page is the click-through rule text for Seeking and Raising posts. It restates the Community Guidelines and the Important Disclosures. Bracketed items are placeholders still to be confirmed.

## What you may post

You may say what role you play, what kind of project or counterparty you are looking for, the market, the asset class, and a size range. You may say what you offer. Keep it factual and professional.

## What you may not post

Nothing on Private Floor is an offer to sell, or a solicitation of an offer to buy, any security or investment. Private Floor does not offer, sell, or recommend securities, and does not solicit investments.

Do not include:

- offering terms, return targets, IRR, a preferred return, an equity multiple, or a percent return;
- a statement that past performance will continue, or any guaranteed result;
- a call for investors, accredited or otherwise, or a subscription;
- an offering memorandum, PPM, or other securities-offering document.

Many private real estate offerings rely on an exemption that prohibits general solicitation. Posting offering terms or return targets to the membership may be treated as general solicitation. You are solely responsible for complying with securities laws, including state law where you and other members are located. Private Floor's membership review is not a pre-existing substantive relationship and is not verification of accredited investor status.

Speak with securities counsel before posting anything about a capital raise. A Private Floor administrator reviews posts that the keyword check holds back. That review is not legal advice and is not an approval of any offering.
