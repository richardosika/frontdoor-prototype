# Private Floor Club - Deal Room NDA Acknowledgement

DRAFT - for attorney review, not legal advice. This is the click-through acknowledgement required before a member enters a deal room. It restates Section 6 of the Terms of Use. Bracketed items are placeholders still to be confirmed.

**Last updated:** [DATE]

A deal room opens to a member only after the member who created it approves access and this acknowledgement is complete. Private Floor records the date, time, account, and version.

Confidential Information means non-public information made available in a deal room, including documents, financial information, project plans, terms, and the identity of parties, whether or not marked confidential. It does not include information that is or becomes public through no fault of yours, that you already lawfully knew, that you lawfully receive from someone else without a confidentiality duty, or that you develop independently.

You will use Confidential Information only to evaluate a possible business relationship with the party that shared it. You will not disclose it except to employees, advisers, and potential financing sources who need it for that purpose and are bound by confidentiality duties at least as protective as these. You will protect it with at least reasonable care. You will not copy or redistribute it except as the deal room permits.

These duties run in favor of the member who shared the information and may be enforced by that member. Private Floor may also enforce them. Private Floor is not a party to your deal, does not review the materials for accuracy, and does not offer securities, take subscriptions, or process payments.

The member who shares materials may require a separate, more detailed NDA. If so, that agreement controls between those parties for its subject matter.
