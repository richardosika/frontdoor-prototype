## 2. Automated verification and public-data checks

This section explains, in plain English, how we verify applicants. Our Verification Policy gives more detail.

### 2.1 What happens when you apply

When you submit an application, the Platform automatically runs checks on you and on the company you list. The checks use **only free, publicly available sources** and the information you give us. We do not buy data from data brokers, we do not use paid background-screening or credit-reporting services, and we do not log in to other websites to view information.

### 2.2 Sources we check

| Check | What we look at |
|---|---|
| Email and domain | whether your email address is valid and deliverable, whether it uses your company's domain or a free or disposable email service, and public domain registration information (such as when a domain was registered) |
| Company website | the public website you list or your email domain points to, to see whether the company appears to exist and whether your name and role are consistent with it |
| Sanctions and watchlists | public government sanctions and watchlists, such as lists published by the U.S. Treasury's Office of Foreign Assets Control (OFAC) and similar government lists |
| SEC EDGAR | public filings with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission that mention you or your company |
| Public court records | public court records, for example through CourtListener, that mention you or your company |
| Public web and news | public search results and news articles about you or your company |
| Public profiles | professional and social profile pages that are publicly visible without logging in (for example, a public LinkedIn page) |
| Business card | if you upload one, we extract the text on the card (optical character recognition) and compare it with your application |
| Duplicates and fraud patterns | whether the application matches an existing or previously removed account, or shows patterns associated with fake or fraudulent applications |

### 2.3 What the checks produce

The checks produce an **internal report**: an internal score, a short summary, the sources consulted, and a recommended outcome (for example, "appears legitimate," "does not appear legitimate," or "needs administrator decision").

### 2.4 A person makes every decision

An administrator of Private Floor (at launch, our owner) reviews every application and every internal report and makes every membership decision. **No membership decision is made solely by automated means.** The administrator may disagree with the recommendation, ask you for more information, or check a source again.

### 2.5 What you and other members see

- The internal report, score, summary, recommendation, and sources are **never shown to other members**.
- Applicants are told only the status of their application: "Verification in progress," "More information needed," "Approved" (with a "Verified" status where applicable), or "Declined." **We do not give reasons for decisions.**
- If a privacy law gives you the right to access personal information we hold about you, we will respond as that law requires, which may include personal information in verification records. We may withhold information where the law allows, for example to protect fraud-prevention and security measures or other people's information.

### 2.6 How to ask us to take another look

If you think a decision was based on wrong or outdated information, email connect@privatefloorclub.com with the subject line **"Verification Review."** Tell us what you believe is wrong and, if you wish, send information that corrects it. A person will re-review your application, which may include re-running checks. We will tell you the outcome (status only). This does not limit any right you have under applicable privacy law (see Section 10).

### 2.7 Not a consumer report

**Private Floor is not a consumer reporting agency.** Verification is used only for our own decision about membership in Private Floor. It is not used, and may not be used, to decide anyone's eligibility for credit, employment, housing or tenancy, insurance, or any similar purpose, and we do not furnish reports about anyone to anyone.

### 2.8 Your consent

We run these checks only after you consent to them in the Applicant Consent and Acknowledgment at signup. We record the date, time, and document versions you accepted. You can withdraw consent by emailing us; we will then stop new checks, but we will not be able to complete your review and may not be able to keep your membership active.

## 5. Moderator and administrator access to messages and content

**Messages on the Platform are not end-to-end encrypted and are not private from Private Floor.** Our authorized moderators and administrators can access and read messages, deal-room content, posts, and other Member Content for:

- safety, moderation, and investigating reports or suspected violations;
- verification of members and projects;
- fraud prevention and security;
- legal compliance and responding to lawful requests; and
- technical support when you ask for help.

Access is limited to people who need it for these purposes, and every access is recorded in an audit log showing who accessed what and when. We do not read messages to sell advertising or to pursue our own business deals.
