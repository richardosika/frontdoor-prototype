## What members see

Other members see your name, role, company, city, and what you post. They do not see your email, phone, LinkedIn URL, business card, references, or verification file.

## What Richard Osika sees

The application, the optional business card, references you choose to give, and an automated verification report. The report uses free public sources. It may be inconclusive when a source is blocked. Members never see the checklist, notes, score, or sources. They see “Verified” or “Verification in progress” on a committee file, and a plain membership status on the application.

## Notices

During this test, outbound email notices go only to the configured test recipient. They are also stored in the admin Outbox. Applicants are not emailed.

## Acceptance

When you accept this policy, Private Floor stores the document name, version, time, and a hash of the IP address. The raw IP address is not stored.
