# Private Floor Club - Verification Policy

DRAFT - for attorney review, not legal advice. Bracketed items are placeholders still to be confirmed.

**Last updated:** [DATE]

This policy explains, in plain English, how Private Floor checks applicants and projects, what "Verified" means, and what it does not mean. It is part of our Terms of Use. The English version governs over any translation.

## 1. Why we verify

Private Floor is a private network where every member is personally reviewed, so members can spend their time with real people at real firms. Anyone can request to join, and every request is personally reviewed. Verification is meant to screen out fake accounts, impersonation, and obvious misrepresentation. It does not replace your own due diligence.

## 2. How it works, in two steps

**Step 1: automated checks.** When you apply, the Platform automatically checks the information you give us against free, public sources and prepares an internal report for our administrator.

**Step 2: a person decides.** An administrator of Private Floor (at launch, our owner) reviews your application and the internal report and makes the decision. **No membership decision is made solely by automated means.** The administrator can disagree with the automated recommendation, ask you for more information, or look at a source again.

## 3. What the automated checks look at

We use **only free, publicly available sources** and the information you give us. We do not buy data from data brokers, use paid background-screening or credit-reporting services, or log in to other websites to view information.

- **Email and domain:** whether your email address works, whether it uses your company's own domain, and public domain registration information (for example, how long the domain has existed).
- **Your company's website:** whether the company appears to exist and whether your name and role are consistent with it.
- **Sanctions and watchlists:** public government lists, such as the sanctions lists published by the U.S. Treasury's Office of Foreign Assets Control (OFAC) and similar government lists.
- **SEC EDGAR:** public filings with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission that mention you or your company.
- **Public court records:** for example, through CourtListener.
- **Public web and news search:** public search results and news articles.
- **Public profile pages:** professional or social profiles that anyone can see without logging in (for example, a public LinkedIn page).
- **Your business card (optional):** if you upload one, we read the text on it automatically and compare it with your application.
- **Duplicates and fraud patterns:** whether the application matches another account or a previously removed account, or looks like a known pattern of fake applications.

The checks also apply to the company you list.

## 4. What the internal report contains

An internal score, a short summary, the sources checked, and a recommended outcome: **appears legitimate**, **does not appear legitimate**, or **needs administrator decision**. The report is an internal tool for our administrator.

## 5. What you will see

We keep the internal report, score, summary, recommendation, and sources confidential. **Other members never see them.** You will see only your application status:

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| Verification in progress | We have your application and are reviewing it. |
| More information needed | We need something more from you (for example, a company website or a business card). We will say what. |
| Approved / Verified | An administrator approved your membership. |
| Declined | Your application was not approved. We do not give reasons. |

If a privacy law gives you a right to access personal information we hold about you, we will respond as that law requires (see our Privacy Policy).

## 6. What you can choose to give us

- **Business card image (optional):** helps us match your name, role, and company. Stored privately, seen only by administrators, never shown to members, and deleted within [30] days after the decision.
- **References (optional):** we may email a reference once (plus one reminder) only to confirm that they know you and that you are who you say you are. You must have their permission first. They receive our Reference-giver Notice.
- **What we do not ask for:** government-issued ID (such as a driver's license or passport), Social Security numbers, dates of birth, professional license documents, or license numbers. Please do not send them.

## 7. Your consent

Before we run any checks, you agree to them in the Applicant Consent and Acknowledgment at signup. We record the date and time and the version of the documents you accepted.

## 8. Not a consumer report

Private Floor is not a consumer reporting agency. Verification is only for our own decision about membership in Private Floor. It is never used, and may not be used by anyone, to decide eligibility for credit, employment, housing or tenancy, insurance, or any similar purpose.

## 9. If you think we got it wrong

Public sources can be wrong or out of date, and automated checks can confuse people or companies with similar names. If you think a decision was based on wrong information, email connect@privatefloorclub.com with the subject line **"Verification Review."** Tell us what you think is wrong and, if you like, send information that corrects it. A person will re-review your application and tell you the outcome (status only).

## 10. Project review

When a member posts a project, an administrator may check whether the member appears to be associated with it and whether basic facts (such as the property address or owner of record) are consistent with free public records. We do **not** review a project's financials, projections, title, environmental condition, zoning, entitlements, construction, legal structure, or securities law compliance, and we do not appraise any property.

## 11. What "Verified" means

"Verified" means that, at the time of review, an administrator approved the member after reviewing the information the member gave us and the results of our checks.

## 12. What verification does NOT mean

Verification is a limited, good-faith review. It is **not**:

- a guarantee of identity, honesty, competence, licensing, solvency, or ability to perform;
- an endorsement or recommendation of any member, project, or investment;
- due diligence on any project, deal, or offering;
- a consumer report, credit report, or criminal records report;
- verification of accredited investor or qualified purchaser status; or
- a determination that any offering complies with law.

Information can change after we check it, people can provide false information, and public sources can be wrong. We may miss things. Do your own independent investigation before relying on anyone or anything on the Platform.

## 13. Members without a Verified status

A member or project without a Verified status may simply not have finished review. That is not a negative finding.

## 14. Re-checks and removal

We may re-run checks at any time, for example after a report, a change in profile, or periodically [every [12] months]. An administrator may remove Verified status, restrict an account, or remove a member if information appears inaccurate or for any reason in our Terms. A person makes every such decision.

## 15. How we protect review records

Internal reports, notes, and audit logs are visible only to authorized administrators, every access is logged, and we keep them only as long as described in our Privacy Policy.
