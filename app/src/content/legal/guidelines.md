# Private Floor Club - Community Guidelines and Code of Conduct

DRAFT - for attorney review, not legal advice. Bracketed items are placeholders still to be confirmed.

**Last updated:** [DATE]

Private Floor is a vetted community of real estate professionals across the United States. Members are here to meet serious counterparties and do good work together. These guidelines describe how we expect members to behave. They are part of our Terms of Use, alongside our Acceptable Use and Abuse Reporting Policy. Breaking them can lead to removal of content, loss of a Verified badge, suspension, or permanent removal.

## 1. Be who you say you are

- Use your real name and accurately describe your company, role, experience, and track record. If you mention a license, it must be one you actually hold.
- Do not claim a title, license, deal, or relationship you do not have.
- Keep your profile current. If you change firms or roles, update it.
- One person, one account. Do not share your login.

## 2. Be honest in what you post

- Post only accurate information that you have the right to share.
- Do not exaggerate, cherry-pick, or leave out facts that make a post misleading.
- Clearly label projections, estimates, and targets as such.
- If you learn something you posted is wrong, correct or remove it promptly.

## 3. Respect confidentiality

- Treat deal-room materials as confidential and follow the Deal Room NDA Acknowledgement and any separate NDA you sign.
- Do not screenshot, forward, or share another member's non-public information outside the Platform without permission.
- Do not post confidential information belonging to your employer, clients, or anyone else unless you are authorized to.

## 4. Keep communication on the Platform and respectful

- All member-to-member communication happens inside the Platform. Other members' email addresses, phone numbers, and LinkedIn profiles are hidden. Do not try to harvest contact details or move people off-platform for unsolicited marketing. If you choose to share your own contact details with someone, that is your decision and your risk.
- Be professional. Disagree on the merits, not the person.
- No harassment, threats, intimidation, hate speech, or discrimination based on race, color, religion, national origin, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, age, disability, familial status, or any other protected characteristic.
- No spam: no bulk or repetitive messages, chain messages, or unsolicited sales pitches to people who have not shown interest.
- Respect "no." If someone declines or blocks you, do not contact them again through other accounts or channels.

## 5. Follow the law and stay within your license

- Follow our Seeking and Raising Post Rules for anything about capital, investors, or financing.
- Do not use the Platform to offer or sell securities in violation of law, or to act as an unregistered broker-dealer, finder, real estate broker, or mortgage broker.
- Do not offer or request referral fees or commissions that require a license you do not hold.
- Comply with federal, state, and local fair housing and anti-discrimination laws in anything you post about properties or tenants.
- Do not use the Platform for fraud, money laundering, bribery, sanctions evasion, or any other illegal activity.
- Do not use the Platform to agree with competitors on prices, bids, wages, or to divide markets or customers.

## 6. Respect the Platform

- Do not scrape, crawl, copy, or export member data or content.
- Do not use bots or automated tools without our written permission.
- Do not try to get around security, access controls, blocks, or removals.
- Do not upload malware or anything designed to harm systems.
- Respect intellectual property. Post only content you own or have permission to use.

## 7. Reporting, blocking, and moderation

- Use the **Report** button to flag content or members you believe break these guidelines or the law. Reports are reviewed by our team. False or bad-faith reports are themselves a violation.
- Use the **Block** button to stop a member from contacting you.
- Our moderators and administrators can read messages and content on the Platform to review reports, verify members, and keep the community safe. Every access is logged. See our Privacy Policy.
- We decide in our discretion what action to take, which may include a warning, content removal, restricted features, loss of Verified badge, suspension, or permanent removal. We may act without advance notice where needed to protect members.
- A person, not an automated system, decides every enforcement action. If you believe we made a mistake, email connect@privatefloorclub.com with the subject line "Moderation Review" within [30] days. We will consider your request, but our decision is final.

## 8. Off-platform conduct

We may consider serious misconduct outside the Platform that appears in public sources, such as fraud findings, regulatory sanctions, or court records relevant to trust, when an administrator decides on membership. See our Verification Policy.

## 8A. Language

You may post in any language, but posts must follow these guidelines in every language. The English version of these guidelines governs.
