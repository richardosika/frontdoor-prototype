# Community Guidelines

**Draft — pending attorney review.**

Private Floor Club is nationwide and invite-only. Richard Osika approves every member. Be specific, be civil, and keep confidential material inside an approved deal room.

## Introductions

Ask before you introduce two members. Say why the introduction fits. Do not forward a deal room file outside the club.

## What to post

Name the market, the role you need, and the size in plain language. Do not promise returns. Do not ask members to wire funds through Private Floor.

## What stays private

Email, phone, LinkedIn, business cards, and verification notes stay off the member feed. A business card is optional and is visible only to Richard Osika.

## Inviting a colleague

Request an invite from the feed. The code is issued inside the admin screen and is not emailed. Share it yourself.

## If something is wrong

Use the report control on a message or a deal. Reports go to Richard Osika. Do not argue them out in the feed.
