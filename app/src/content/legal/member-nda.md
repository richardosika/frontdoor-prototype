# Private Floor Club - Member Non-Disclosure and Non-Circumvention Agreement

DRAFT - pending review by a New Jersey attorney. Not legal advice. This draft is not final.

**Last updated:** [DATE]

This agreement is between you and Private Floor Club LLC ("Private Floor"), and it protects every other member. It applies to everyone who uses the Private Floor Club as a member.

## What is confidential

Confidential information means anything you receive through the Private Floor Club that is not already public, including:

- deal information: projects, properties, addresses, pricing, terms, capital needs, and timelines;
- documents: offering materials, rent rolls, budgets, plans, financial statements, and anything uploaded to a project or deal room;
- deal rooms and everything in them;
- messages and conversations between members;
- member details: names, companies, roles, contact information, track records, photos, and who is working with whom.

## What you agree to

- Use confidential information only to evaluate or pursue opportunities with the member who shared it, through the Private Floor Club.
- Do not share, forward, copy, screenshot for others, post, or disclose confidential information outside the platform, to anyone, without the written consent of the member who shared it.
- You may share it with your own attorneys, accountants, lenders, or advisors who need it for that evaluation and who are bound to keep it confidential. You are responsible for them.
- Protect confidential information with at least reasonable care.
- If the law requires you to disclose something, tell the member who shared it (if legally allowed) so they can respond, and disclose only what is required.

## Non-circumvention

You will not go around the member who brought a deal, project, or contact to you through the Private Floor Club. That means you will not contact, deal with, or close a transaction directly or indirectly with the owner, sponsor, seller, lender, investor, or other party introduced through that member in order to avoid, reduce, or exclude that member's role, fee, or participation, unless that member agrees in writing. This applies for 24 months after you first received the information or introduction.

## What is not covered

Information is not confidential if you can show it was already public, was already lawfully known to you, was independently developed by you, or was received from someone free to share it.

## How long it lasts

These obligations start when you accept this agreement and survive after you leave the Private Floor Club, are removed, or cancel your membership. Confidentiality lasts for 3 years after you last received the information, and for trade secrets for as long as they remain trade secrets. Non-circumvention lasts for the 24-month period above.

## If this agreement is broken

A breach can cause harm that money alone cannot fix. The member who shared the information, and Private Floor, may seek an injunction in addition to any other remedy. Private Floor may suspend or remove a member who breaks this agreement.

## Other terms

Private Floor does not take part in members' deals and is not a broker, adviser, or party to them. This agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New Jersey. It works together with the Member Terms and the Deal Room NDA Acknowledgement; where they differ, the stricter protection for the member who shared the information applies. Members who share information are intended beneficiaries and may enforce this agreement. If any part is unenforceable, the rest still applies.
