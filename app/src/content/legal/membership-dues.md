# Private Floor Club - Membership Dues and Refund Terms

DRAFT - for attorney review, not legal advice. This page restates Section 8 of the Terms of Use. Bracketed items are placeholders still to be confirmed.

**Last updated:** [DATE]

## Monthly dues

Paid memberships are $199 per month; the first month is $99.50.

Membership is free for developers, lenders, family offices, and investors/LPs.

Private Floor decides the category from the professional role on the approved application. Monthly dues are due only after a Private Floor administrator approves the application. The next charge after the first month is the full monthly price, one month later. A reminder goes out before that full-price charge.

The English text governs if a translation differs.

## Month to month

Membership is month to month. There is no long-term contract. You can cancel anytime on the payment page or in Settings, or by emailing connect@privatefloorclub.com. Cancellation stops the next charge. If the current month is already paid, access continues until that month ends. An administrator can waive dues for a person. A waiver is not automatic.

## What the fee covers

The fee is one month of access to the Private Floor Club. Private Floor does not charge referral fees, finder's fees, commissions, success fees, carried interest, or any other transaction-based compensation. Private Floor does not hold, receive, or move members' money or securities, and does not process payments between members.

## Cancellation

You can cancel on the payment page or in Settings, after a confirmation step. Cancellation stops future charges. Access continues until the end of the period already paid.

## Pausing a membership

A paid member can pause the membership for 1 to 6 months in Settings. Nothing is charged while the membership is paused. A reminder email goes out one week before it resumes. If you take no action, the membership resumes automatically at the full price of $199 per month.

## Payment methods

You can keep a primary card and one backup card. If a charge on the primary card fails, the backup card is charged. Apple Pay and Google Pay are offered where your device supports them. Card details are entered into Stripe; Private Floor stores only the card brand and last four digits that Stripe returns.

## Refunds

Cancellation stops the next monthly charge. A charge already recorded for the current month is not returned automatically. A refund is recorded by a Private Floor administrator. Private Floor does not store a card number.

## How payment is collected

Card details, when Stripe test mode is used, are entered into Stripe. Private Floor does not store full card numbers. Checkout runs in Stripe test mode when test keys are configured. When they are not, the payment page runs a simulated payment that is labelled TEST and does not charge a card. No live card is charged. We will tell you before any live charge begins.
