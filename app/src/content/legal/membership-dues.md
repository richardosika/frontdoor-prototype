## Free memberships

Membership is free for members approved as developers, investors and limited partners, family offices, and lenders. Approval activates that membership. No dues are collected.

## Annual dues

Members approved in these categories pay a flat annual fee, billed once a year:

- General contractors: $2,400 per year
- Brokers: $1,800 per year
- Attorneys and advisors: $1,200 per year

Private Floor decides the category from the professional role on the approved application. Annual dues are due only after a Private Floor administrator approves the application. They are never based on a deal. The renewal date is one year after the annual dues are recorded.

## What the fee covers

The fee is one year of access to the Private Floor Club. It is a flat membership fee. It is never based on, and never changes because of, whether any deal, investment, capital raise, loan, sale, lease, or other transaction happens between members, or its size or terms. Private Floor does not charge referral fees, finder's fees, commissions, success fees, carried interest, or any other transaction-based compensation. Private Floor does not hold, receive, or move members' money or securities, and does not process payments between members.
