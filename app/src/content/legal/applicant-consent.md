**Before you submit**

Private Floor is a vetted network. To review your application:

- **We run automated checks using free, public information only.** These include your email and company domain (including public domain registration information), your company's public website, government sanctions and watchlists (such as OFAC lists), SEC EDGAR filings, public court records, public web and news search, and profile pages that are publicly visible without logging in. If you upload a business card, we read its text and compare it with your application. We also check for duplicate or fraudulent applications.
- **A person decides.** The checks produce an internal score, summary, and recommendation. Our administrator reviews them and makes every membership decision. No decision is made by automated means alone.
- **What you will see.** We will tell you your status: Verification in progress, More information needed, Approved, or Declined. We do not share the internal report or give reasons. If you think we relied on wrong information, email connect@privatefloorclub.com with the subject line "Verification Review."
- **Not a consumer report.** Private Floor is not a consumer reporting agency. These checks are used only to decide membership in Private Floor, never for credit, employment, housing, or insurance decisions.
- **Optional items.** A business card and references are optional. We may email references only to confirm they know you. We never ask for government ID or license numbers.
- **Communication and privacy.** All member communication happens inside the Platform. Other members do not see your email, phone, LinkedIn, or business card. Moderators and administrators can read messages for safety and verification, and every access is logged.
